NUGGET BFSI OUTREACH PLAN: 40 CXO MEETINGS IN 4 WEEKS

THE MATH
Cold outreach to CXOs converts at 1-3%, so cold alone won't get to 40. Aim for 55 booked to cover no-shows:
- 15 from warm intros (Zomato/Eternal network, investors, Nugget customers)
- 15 from value-first cold outreach
- 10 from events, CXO roundtables and LinkedIn content

TARGETS
About 70 accounts: the top 25 banks and about 45 insurers, ranked into 3 tiers.
5-7 people at each: CEO, COO, Chief Customer Officer, CDO/CIO, Head of Contact Centre, Head of Digital (about 400 contacts).

THE HOOK: A FREE AI SUPPORT AUDIT
An AI agent tests each bank's or insurer's chatbot, IVR and WhatsApp with 30 real customer queries in English, Hindi and regional languages. The CXO gets a one-page scorecard: "Your bot resolved 9 of 30." It's specific, costs them nothing, and points straight at what Nugget fixes.
AI stack: Sales Navigator/Apollo/Clay for lists, Claude/ChatGPT for account briefs (annual reports, RBI and IRDAI complaint data), LLM-drafted messages edited by a person, Loom videos, Instantly for sequencing, HubSpot as CRM.

WEEK BY WEEK
Week 1 - Build: warm new email domains, build the list, audit the top 30 accounts, send warm-intro asks. Target: 8 meetings
Week 2 - Launch: tier 1 sequence (email + scorecard, LinkedIn, Loom, call); founders post audit insights. Target: 20 total
Week 3 - Scale: tier 2 sequence, reach several people per account, roundtable 1. Target: 32 total
Week 4 - Close: roundtable 2, re-engage warm leads, ask every meeting for a referral. Target: 40+ total

GUARDRAILS
- A meeting counts only with a CXO or their named delegate and a clear next step.
- Follow DPDP and TRAI DND rules; keep email volume low.
- Track progress daily. If behind after week 2, move effort to warm intros and roundtables.
- From Nugget: 2 hours a week of founder time, one customer metric to cite, and a budget for tools and events.
